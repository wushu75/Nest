Everything Etsy should have built in

Nest adds the collection, sharing, and discovery tools that Etsy's been missing. Runs entirely in your browser.

One-click save

A save button appears on every Etsy listing card. Press N to quick-save the item you're looking at. Create colour-coded nests to keep things sorted.

Share with anyone

Send a link that opens a beautiful viewer in any browser — no extension needed. Or paste a share code. Your data never touches a server.

Mood boards

Drag saved items onto a free-form canvas. Rotate, layer, add colour swatches and notes. Export a crisp 2× PNG when you're happy with it.

Style matching

Nest learns your taste from what you save and browse — entirely on your device. Get personalised Etsy search suggestions and gift ideas for the people in your life.

Buyer insights

See review summaries, shipping speed, eco-friendliness, and shop signals right on the listing page. Make decisions without scrolling through hundreds of reviews.

Price drop alerts

Nest checks your saved items every few hours. If something drops in price or comes back in stock, you get a notification. No email sign-ups, no noise.

Sharing that respects your privacy

Most extensions phone home when you share. Nest takes a different approach: your data is compressed and encoded directly into the link itself.

1

Pack — your nest is compressed into a compact array

2

Compress — the browser's built-in deflate shrinks it further

3

Fragment — the data goes in the URL after the #, which browsers never send to servers

The same data also works as a pasteable nest1. share code — no link required.

Private by design, not by promise

Nest stores everything in your browser's IndexedDB. There's no account, no telemetry, no analytics. The code is open source and you can read every line.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all data. You can also export everything as JSON first.
